# Receipts

A smart, AI-powered budgeting app that takes the friction out of personal finance through receipt scanning and voice input.

## Why I Built This
I was tired of manually typing every single expense into a spreadsheet at the end of the month. I wanted a smart budget tracker where I could simply snap a picture of a receipt or use my voice to quickly log an expense, and have the system parse the details automatically.

This app allows me to track my budget visually, manage my recurring subscriptions, add and track my income, and get insights into my spending habits using natural language search. It supports multiple currencies and makes tracking finances effortless instead of a chore.

## Features
- **📸 AI Receipt Analysis**: Take a photo of a receipt, and the app automatically extracts the total, date, and categorizes the expense.
- **🎤 Voice Input**: Simply speak your expense (e.g., "Spent 15 dollars on lunch yesterday") and watch it get logged instantly.
- **💰 Income & Subscriptions**: Track your income streams and manage all your recurring subscriptions in one place.
- **🔍 Natural Language Search**: Ask questions about your spending to get instant insights.
- **💱 Multi-currency Support**: Automatically handles different currencies for seamless tracking.
